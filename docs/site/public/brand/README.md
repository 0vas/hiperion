# Hyperion identity

Original SVG artwork for this repository, under its MIT license. A compact H monogram sits inside an open solar halo. Its central point represents light and the decision that connects two sides of a workflow. The open arcs leave room for an evolving plan. The mark does not trace or reproduce the supplied stock illustration.

- `../logo.svg`: square monogram, navigation mark and documentation favicon. Works at 24 px.
- `hyperion-solar.svg`: transparent illustration of the same mark.
- `hyperion-banner.svg`: README lockup on a midnight background.

Palette: midnight `#17252b`, solar gold `#b88a43`, ivory `#f7f3e9`. Keep clear space around the mark, do not animate or stretch it, and retain descriptive alternative text. The symbol always appears beside the Hyperion name in navigation. These assets do not change icons inside already released installers.

Platform icons in `PlatformIcon.vue` identify macOS and Windows. They are local SVG silhouettes, separate from the Hyperion identity; platform names remain visible. Their use does not imply endorsement.
