# Community code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome questions, provide constructive technical feedback, and make room for different experiences and perspectives.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publishing private information, and unwanted sexual attention are unacceptable. Keep discussion relevant to the project and criticize ideas rather than people.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when necessary. They should explain decisions privately where possible, act proportionately, and protect reporters' privacy. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through a contact channel on their GitHub profile; avoid publishing personal details in issues.

This policy applies in the repository and other project-managed community spaces.
